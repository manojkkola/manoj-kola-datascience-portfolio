About

I'm Manoj Kola, a Lead Data Engineer with 16+ years of progressive experience designing enterprise-grade ETL/ELT pipelines and leading engineering teams across financial services and healthcare. I first stepped into team leadership managing an offshore engineering team at Nationwide Insurance, and I've been leading teams ever since — most notably as Technical Anchor across a 30-person, 5-team organization at Discover Financial Services, directly managing 6 engineers while setting architecture standards and driving cross-team delivery, and now leading engineering delivery at Capital One.
